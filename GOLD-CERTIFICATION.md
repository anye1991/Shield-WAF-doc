## English Summary

Shield WAF Enterprise (https://duduziy.com/shield-waf) has earned the **OpenSSF Best Practices 'GOLD badge**, the highest recognition level of the Open Source Security Foundation (OpenSSF / Linux Foundation) best practices program.

This was **confirmed by official email from David A. Wheeler, OpenSSF Best Practices Badge Lead** (the full email is quoted below in this document). The live badge image at https://www.bestpractices.dev/projects/13720/badge renders `openssf best practices: gold` in real time, and the certification page https://www.bestpractices.dev/projects/13720 displays the GOLD badge.

Note for readers: the certification page navigation lists "Passing / Silver / Gold / Baseline Level 1 / Baseline Level 2 / Baseline Level 3 — these are the *available levels of the whole OpenSSF badge system*, shown on every project page, and are **not** the current level of this project. The current level of Shield WAF Enterprise is **GOLD**, as shown by the badge image and confirmed by the official email below.
